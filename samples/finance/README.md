# Fictional company finance pack

All company names, customers, numbers, dates, and business assumptions are synthetic. Currency: USD. Reporting period: Q3 2026; plans and pipeline: Q4 / October 2026.

- quarterly-financial-report.pdf: four-page management report covering operating results, cash flow, receivables, forecasts, budget, and pipeline.
- finance-team-memo.pdf: one-page internal memo with operational priorities.
- monthly-revenue.csv: three monthly observations; compatible with the current app's upload format.
- quarterly-profit-and-loss.csv: revenue, cost of revenue, expense categories, and operating profit.
- monthly-cash-flow.csv: collections, payments, opening cash, and closing cash.
- october-sales-pipeline.csv: six fictional opportunities with assumed probabilities and weighted contract values.
- q4-budget.csv: separate management plan for October–December.
- HTML files: printable source for the PDFs.

The document workspace accepts these PDFs, CSVs, and HTML files. Upload one or several and ask what to summarize, compare, extract, or visualize. PDFs are read by Gemini on send; CSV/HTML are passed as text. The model must distinguish actuals, budgets, bookings, and estimates.

Reconciled totals: revenue $1,240,000; cost of revenue $372,000; gross profit $868,000; operating expenses $519,000; operating profit $349,000. Opening cash $500,000 + net cash increase $168,000 = closing cash $668,000. Receivables: $150,000 opening + $1,240,000 revenue - $1,160,000 collections = $230,000 closing. Accounts payable and other full balance-sheet disclosures are outside scope.

The P&L excludes interest, income-tax expense, depreciation, and amortization. Cash taxes are not P&L tax expense. Pipeline bookings are not recognized revenue. October's statistical estimate ($543,333.33) and budget ($520,000) are deliberately different; neither is an actual result. Linear regression over only three months is an illustrative baseline, not a reliable financial forecast.
