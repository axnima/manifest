The quarter at a glance

  • Monthly revenue rose from $350,000 in July to $480,000 in September, an increase of 37.1%.
  • Cost of revenue was 30% of revenue in each month, leaving $868,000 in gross profit for the quarter.

  • Customer collections totaled $1.16M; closing accounts receivable was $230,000.
A useful distinction
Revenue records sales earned. Cash collections record payments received. Neither is interchangeable with new sales bookings.
